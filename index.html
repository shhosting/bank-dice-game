    How to play
    • Each round starts with a shared Bank of 0. Players take turns rolling two dice; every roll changes the Bank for everyone.
    • Rolls 1–3 of a round: rolling a 7 adds 70 to the Bank. Any other roll, including doubles, just adds the sum.
    • Roll 4 onward: rolling a 7 busts the round — the Bank drops to 0 and anyone who hasn't banked yet scores 0. Rolling doubles doubles the current Bank.
    • At any point, a player can hit Bank to lock in the current Bank total as their score for the round, then sit out until the next round.
    • The round ends when everyone has banked, or a bust happens. Highest total after all rounds wins.
